Co-founded SweetBeasts Inc., a charitable venture designing and selling fruit-animal plush toys. As CTO and COO, I led a team of three to build our own e-commerce platform from scratch instead of relying on off-the-shelf templates.

## What it does

The storefront handles browsing, checkout, and fulfillment workflows with features like remote cart management and partial refunds. Alongside the customer-facing site, an integrated admin portal streamlines day-to-day operations across products, orders, coupons, affiliate tracking, and customer support.

## Under the hood

Built with **Next.js**, **React**, and **Tailwind CSS**, backed by **Prisma** for database access and **Stripe** for payment processing and refunds. Server Actions power product and order management in the admin portal so updates reflect immediately across the storefront.
